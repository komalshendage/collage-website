Welcome to Shriram Institute Of Engineering & Technology (Poly), Paniv. The institute offers diploma-level engineering education focused on practical knowledge, modern technology, and overall student development.

We aim to create skilled engineers who are technically sound, socially responsible, and capable of meeting industry demands.

About Us

Shriram Institute Of Engineering & Technology (Poly), Paniv is committed to delivering technical excellence through quality education, experienced faculty, and well-equipped laboratories.

The institute encourages innovation, ethical values, teamwork, and continuous learning to prepare students for higher education and professional careers.

Courses Offered

The institute offers diploma programs designed according to current industry standards and technical requirements.

  • Diploma in Computer Engineering – Software, programming & IT skills
  • Diploma in Civil Engineering – Construction & infrastructure development
  • Diploma in Mechanical Engineering – Manufacturing & mechanical systems
  • Diploma in Electrical Engineering – Power systems & electrical machines
  • Diploma in E&TC Engineering – Electronics & communication technologies

Engineering Departments

💻 Computer Engineering

The Computer Engineering department focuses on programming languages, software development, database management, networking, cybersecurity, and modern web technologies with practical lab sessions.

🏗️ Civil Engineering

The Civil Engineering department covers construction technology, surveying, structural analysis, building planning, material testing, and infrastructure project management.

⚙️ Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering deals with machines, manufacturing processes, automobile systems, thermal engineering, CAD/CAM, and workshop practices.

⚡ Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering includes electrical machines, power generation, transmission systems, wiring practices, renewable energy sources, and industrial electrical applications.

📡 E&TC Engineering

Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering focuses on electronic circuits, communication systems, microcontrollers, IoT applications, embedded systems, and PCB designing techniques.
